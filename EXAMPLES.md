# Questions to try

Select the installed GuildQuality Analyze Feedback skill in your approved AI chat or project. Use your own GuildQuality export or a checked account connection. Keep local exports and reports in your private reports folder.

## Start with your export

Upload an export your company allows you to share with the AI service, then ask:

> Use the GuildQuality feedback-analysis skill with my export. First check which dates, locations, survey questions, rating scales, import flags, and record IDs it contains. Explain what is missing and what you can answer. Then summarize the completed survey feedback the file supports. State the period, time zone, response counts, exclusions, and missing data. Show source IDs for examples. Use only this file.

## Start with a connected account

After private setup, ask:

> Use my saved GuildQuality connection to check current access to our approved account and locations. Summarize completed survey feedback for last month. State the dates, time zone, locations, response counts, exclusions, and missing data. Show source survey IDs for examples.

## Explore your feedback

Choose questions that the available records can answer:

- “How did our customer feedback change last month? Show how many responses were used and compare with the previous full month.”
- “Which similar branches need a closer look? Show response counts and flag small samples or missing location details.”
- “Which complaints are becoming more common? Show the number of comments, how you counted them, and examples with source IDs.”
- “Did our company score change because branches scored differently, or because we heard from a different mix of branches?”
- “Compare our divisions from the start of our fiscal year through today. Confirm the dates and which survey questions can be combined.”
- “Do we have the send dates, statuses, and completed responses needed to calculate a response rate for surveys sent last month? If so, calculate it and state when responses were observed.”

Use exact dates for a report you can repeat. Scores for completed surveys and response rates for surveys sent use different groups. Ask for source IDs and data coverage when checking an unexpected result. See [Using GuildQuality Skills](USAGE.md) for more tips.
